AI Overviews and Small Businesses: How to Keep Getting Found

Google's AI answers are changing search. Here is what small businesses can do to stay visible and keep winning customers.

Nadeem Mayo·April 10, 2026

Google's AI Overviews now answer many questions directly in search. If your site relied on simple informational content, you may already have seen fewer clicks. The good news is that most small businesses earn their customers from searches AI can't fully replace.

Focus on searches that lead to a sale

People searching for “emergency plumber near me”, “best bookkeeper for contractors” or “buy handmade oak table” want to take action. These commercial and local searches still send plenty of clicks, so make sure your service and product pages are the strongest pages on your site.

Show real experience

AI tends to summarise what is already common knowledge. Content that shows your own photos, prices, process and customer questions gives Google a reason to cite you and gives readers a reason to visit.

  • Add short case examples to service pages.
  • Answer the questions customers actually ask on the phone.
  • Use your own images instead of stock photos.

Keep your Google Business Profile busy

For local businesses, the map results remain one of the biggest sources of calls. Fresh photos, accurate services and steady genuine reviews keep you competitive.

Earn a few strong mentions

A handful of links and mentions from respected sites in your industry still signal trust. Quality matters far more than quantity.

AI changes how people search, not why they buy. Be the most helpful, trustworthy option and you'll keep winning.
