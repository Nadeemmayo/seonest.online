Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

SEONest (seonest.online) is operated by Nadeem Mayo from Lahore, Pakistan, and serves clients in India. You can reach us at ceo@seonest.online or on WhatsApp at +923074646927.

What we collect

When you fill in a form, email us or message us on WhatsApp we receive the details you choose to share: usually your name, email, company, website, the services you are interested in and your message.

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It collects anonymised usage data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver work you order, send invoices and keep basic business records. We do not sell or rent personal data and we do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Who we share it with

We share the minimum needed with services that help us run the business: our form provider (FormSubmit), email and messaging providers, Google Analytics and payment providers. Publishers only receive the content and target URL needed to publish an order, never your contact details unless you ask us to.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months. Order and invoice records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to delete your data sooner where the law allows.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data and withdraw consent. You can also raise a grievance with us by email, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India where applicable. To make a request, email ceo@seonest.online and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We use analytics cookies from Google. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site still works without them.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
