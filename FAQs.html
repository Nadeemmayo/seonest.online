FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

Everything business owners usually ask us about SEO, websites, ads and working with SEONest.

Working with SEONest

Who do you usually work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses, online stores, startups and marketing agencies, mostly in the US, UK and Canada.

What does the free growth plan include?

A review of your website, rankings and competitors, plus a short list of the actions likely to make the biggest difference.

Will I have one point of contact?

Yes. You'll have a direct line to the people working on your account.

SEO & links

Are your links safe for my site?

Yes. We only use real websites with organic traffic and relevant content, never private networks or link farms.

How long do guest posts take?

Usually 5–10 working days after you approve the content.

What if a link disappears?

We replace removed links within the 6-month guarantee period at no cost.

How long does SEO take?

Expect early improvements in 2–3 months and stronger results by months 4–6, depending on competition.

Websites

How much does a website cost?

It depends on pages and features. After a short call we send a fixed quote so there are no surprises.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. We build on easy platforms and show you how to make updates.

Do you host websites?

We can, through an optional care plan that covers hosting, backups and updates.

Ads

Which platforms do you run ads on?

Google, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, managed through Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend goes directly to the platform from your account; our fee covers management.

Do I own my ad accounts?

Always. Everything runs in your accounts.

Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?

PayPal, Payoneer, Wise and major credit and debit cards.

Do you offer white-label services?

Yes, for agencies, with unbranded reports and full confidentiality.

Message us and we'll answer within a few hours.
